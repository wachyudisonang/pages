How you learn

Four skills, taught as one method

Most courses teach one thing at a time — a vocabulary drill, then a grammar rule. Our communicative method works all your skills at once, inside real conversation, because that is how a language is actually used. The goal is being understood, not being perfect.

🗣️

Speaking

You speak from lesson one — real dialogue with your tutor, bringing real-life communication into every session.

👂

Listening

Trained ears through natural, conversational-pace practice, so the language you learn is the language you will hear.

📖

Reading & writing

Grammar explained through real usage, reinforced by writing and reading in context — not abstract rules.

🌏

Culture

Cultural context sits inside the conversation, so you learn why someone would say a thing, not just how.

Before you start

Questions people ask first

How soon can I hold a conversation?

Because you speak from lesson one, most learners manage everyday situations within a few months — the pace depends on the language and how often you practise. Many of our students report real daily-life conversation within a year.

Where do lessons take place?

At your home or office in the Jakarta–Depok area, or online — whatever fits your schedule. We arrange the time and place around you.

Which languages do you teach?

Bahasa Indonesia for foreigners, English, German, Mandarin, French, Dutch and Japanese — for all levels, from beginner to business and diplomatic.

Do you teach companies and teams?

Yes. We run in-house corporate training and have taught teams at ExxonMobil, Siemens, Merck, Standard Chartered and many more. Message us for a custom program.

How do I get started?

Just message us on WhatsApp for a free consultation — no obligation. We'll match you with the right tutor and set up a free trial lesson.
